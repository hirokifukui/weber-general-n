Take $n=7$, $m=64$, $k_7=h_7/h_6$. By Lemma~\ref{lem:B}, $d_7(\ell)=1$ for $\ell\equiv1\pmod{128}$ and $d_7(\ell)=2$ for $\ell\equiv63,65,127\pmod{128}$. Lemma~\ref{lem:C7} encloses $C_7$ in a rigorous ball whose upper end is below the integer $T_1=1727342163036353095979941756929$, and $T_2=1314283897427172$ satisfies $T_1<\ell^2$ whenever $T_2<\ell$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:C7int}, kernel-checked; the comparison $C_7<T_1$ and $T_2{}^2$ against the ball are verifier step 04). Hence $\ell>T_1$ gives $\ell^{1}>C_7$, and $\ell>T_2$ with $d_7(\ell)=2$ gives $\ell^2>T_1>C_7$; in both cases Theorem~\ref{thm:A} yields $\ell\nmid k_7$.
