Assume $\ell\mid k_n$. By the first hypothesis some component $f$ is saturated. By the covering hypothesis $f$ carries a RHO witness or a T witness. In the first case the RHO hypothesis says a RHO witness on $f$ refutes saturation of $f$; in the second case the T hypothesis does the same. Either way $f$ is not saturated, a contradiction; so $\ell\nmid k_n$ (\path{exclusion_chain_direct}, a proof by cases with no axiom). The two refutation hypotheses are themselves discharged from the floors: if saturation of $f$ forces $L\le\operatorname{ht}(v)$ (resp.\ $\bar T\le T(v)$) for every nonzero $v$ on $f$, then a nonzero $v$ with $\operatorname{ht}(v)<L$ (resp.\ $T(v)<\bar T$) is incompatible with saturation (\path{rho_witness_refutes_saturation}, \path{t_witness_refutes_saturation}: $a\le b$ and $b<a$ cannot both hold). Finally \path{exclusion_of_checker_spec} assembles these with the specification hypothesis ``the checker's EXCLUDED verdict implies that every component has a nonzero vector with $\operatorname{ht}<L$ or one with $T<\bar T$'': feeding that hypothesis and the two floors into the chain gives $\ell\nmid k_n$ from the verdict. The correspondence between the verdict of the actual script and this predicate is the specification of Definition~\ref{def:verifier}; it is a hypothesis here, not a Lean fact.
