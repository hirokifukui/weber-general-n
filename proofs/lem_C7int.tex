The degree-$1$ statement is the hypothesis itself ($\ell^1=\ell$). For degree $2$: write $T_2=1314283897427172$. If $\ell>T_2$ then $\ell\ge T_2+1$, so $(T_2+1)^2\le\ell^2$ by monotonicity of squaring on $\mathbb N$, and
\[ 1314283897427173^2=1727342163036359799448838771929>1727342163036353095979941756929 \]
by exact integer multiplication; hence $1727342163036353095979941756929<\ell^2$. The real-valued form used in Corollary~\ref{cor:7} follows by casting $\mathbb N\to\mathbb R$.
