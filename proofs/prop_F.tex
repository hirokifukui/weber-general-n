Apply Corollary~\ref{cor:SHmod} (the module form of Theorem~\ref{thm:SH}) with $K=B_n$, $N=2^n$, $G=G_n$, $R=R_n=\Z[x]/(x^m+1)$ (the quotient of $\Z[G_n]$ by $1+\tau$, which acts on $A_n$ as the norm to $B_{n-1}$, trivial on $\RE\supseteq A_n$), $U=\RE$, $A=A_n$, $\varepsilon=\varepsilon_n$ (so $u_a$ is the unit $\prod_i\sigma^i(\varepsilon_n)^{a_i}$ of the setting; $A_n/\{\pm1\}$ is free on $\sigma^i\varepsilon_n$, \cite{KY} \S4, hence cyclic over $R_n$), (Rank) by Lemma~\ref{lem:D}, $D=D_n$, $M=M_{f_0}$ of dimension $d_{f_0}=\deg f_0$, $L=L_{f_0}$ with (Index) by Lemma~\ref{lem:C}, and $L_0=L_n$. Hypothesis (Sat) is Lemma~\ref{lem:E}: for $a\in L_{f_0}\setminus\ell R_n$ the real root $r_a\in\RE$ satisfies $r_a^{\ell}=u_a$ (the base branch $a\in\ell R_n$ of Corollary~\ref{cor:SHmod} is Lemma~\ref{lem:Eprime}). Hypothesis (Floor) is Lemma~\ref{lem:Aplus} (Lemma~\ref{lem:A} with \cite{MO16} Lemma~2.5(1)). Hypothesis (Bl) is \cite{Bl14} Thm.~II in the form \cite{MO16} Thm.~2.7. Corollary~\ref{cor:SHmod} gives $\ell^{d_{f_0}}\le K_mD_n/L_n^m=C_n$.

\emph{Unfolded, for the reader who wants the argument in place:} $\Lambda_{f_0}=\ell^{-1}H_n(L_{f_0})$ has rank $m$ and covolume $D_n/\ell^{d_0}$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:D}); if $\ell^{d_0}>C_n$, Blichfeldt gives $0\ne v=\ell^{-1}H_na\in\Lambda_{f_0}$ with $|v|^m\le(2/\pi)^{m/2}\Gamma(2+m/2)D_n/\ell^{d_0}\le C_nL_n^m/\ell^{d_0}<L_n^m$; if $a\in\ell R_n$, Lemma~\ref{lem:Eprime} makes $v$ the log vector of $u_b\in\RE\setminus\{\pm1\}$, if $a\notin\ell R_n$, Lemma~\ref{lem:E} makes $v$ the log vector of $r_a\in\RE\setminus\{\pm1\}$; Lemma~\ref{lem:Aplus} gives $\hgt\ge L_n$, contradicting $|v|<L_n$.
