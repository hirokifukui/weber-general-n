$\bar T_n$ is defined by cases on $n$: $\bar T_2=17\cdot2^2=68$ and $\bar T_n=33\cdot2^n$ for $n\ge3$, the two literature trace floors on $\RE\setminus\{\pm1\}$ (\cite{MO3} Prop.~6.6 for every $n\ge2$; \cite{KY} Thm.~2.3 for $n\ge3$). The values $\bar T_2=68$ and $\bar T_7=4224$ are read off the definition. For every $n$, $\bar T_n\le33\cdot2^n$ since $17<33$, with strict inequality exactly at $n=2$; so a T~line accepted under the piecewise threshold is accepted under the uniform one, and the converse fails at $n=2$ for $68\le T<132$, which is why the uniform constant cannot be used in Theorem~\ref{thm:cert}. The refutation of saturation by a T~witness is then the abstract statement of Theorem~\ref{thm:certcore} instantiated at $\mathrm{bar}=\bar T_n$: if saturation of $f$ forces $T(v)\ge\bar T_n$ on every nonzero $v$ (the floor hypothesis), a nonzero $v$ with $T(v)<\bar T_n$ contradicts it.
