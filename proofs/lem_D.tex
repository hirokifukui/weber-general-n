(i) If $H_na=0$, every real embedding of $u_a$ has absolute value $1$; since $B_n$ is totally real, every conjugate of $u_a$ lies in $\{\pm1\}$, so the minimal polynomial of $u_a$ over $\Q$, being irreducible with all its roots in $\{\pm1\}$, is $x-1$ or $x+1$, and $u_a=\pm1$. (This is the totally real case of Kronecker's theorem---an algebraic integer all of whose conjugates have absolute value $1$ is a root of unity; the general theorem is not needed.) freeness of $A_n/\{\pm1\}$ (\cite{KY} \S4) forces $a=0$. (ii) $H_n(\Z^m)$ is the image of $A_n\subseteq E_n$ under the logarithmic embedding, whose image of $E_n$ is discrete (Dirichlet); a subgroup of a discrete group is discrete. (iii) A discrete torsion-free subgroup of $\R^{2m}$ is free on $\R$-linearly independent generators; by (i) its $\Z$-rank is $m$, so its $\R$-rank is $m$. Antiperiodicity $\lambda_{j+m}=-\lambda_j$ gives $(H_na)_{j+m}=-(H_na)_j$, so $H_n^{\mathsf T}H_n=2W_n^{\mathsf T}W_n$ and $D_n=\sqrt{\det H_n^{\mathsf T}H_n}=2^{m/2}|\det W_n|$. For $\Lambda_f$: $L_f$ has index $\ell^{m-d_f}$ in $\Z^m$ (Lemma~C), so $H_n(L_f)$ has covolume $D_n\ell^{m-d_f}$, and scaling by $\ell^{-1}$ divides the covolume of a rank-$m$ lattice by $\ell^m$.
