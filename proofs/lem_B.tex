$x^m+1=\Phi_{2^n}(x)$ (the $2^n$-th cyclotomic polynomial: $\Phi_{2^{k+1}}(x)=\Phi_2(x^{2^k})=x^{2^k}+1$); every root $\zeta$ has exact order $2^n$, and $x^m+1$ is separable over $\F_\ell$ because $\ell\nmid2^n$. Over $\F_\ell$ ($\ell$ odd, unramified) the Frobenius $\zeta\mapsto\zeta^\ell$ permutes the roots, and the degree of the factor through $\zeta$ is the least $k$ with $\zeta^{\ell^k}=\zeta$, i.e.\ $\ell^k\equiv1\pmod{2^n}$, the same for every root. At $n=7$, $(\Z/128)^\times$ has exponent $32$; $\ell\equiv1$ has order $1$; $63^2=3969=31\cdot128+1$, $65^2=4225=33\cdot128+1$, $127\equiv-1$ have order $2$.
