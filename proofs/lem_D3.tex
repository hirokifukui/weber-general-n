For $\chi$ even and primitive of conductor $f>1$ (\cite{Wash}, Chapter~4), and with $|\tau(\chi)|=\sqrt f$,
\[ L(1,\chi)=-\frac{\tau(\chi)}{f}\sum_{a=1}^f\bar\chi(a)\log|1-\zeta_f^a| . \]
Applying this to $\bar\chi$ and using $|L(1,\bar\chi)|=|L(1,\chi)|$:
\[ \Big|\sum_{a\in(\Z/f)^\times}\chi(a)\log|1-\zeta_f^a|\Big|=\sqrt f\,|L(1,\chi)| . \] The conjugation convention does not affect the absolute value.
