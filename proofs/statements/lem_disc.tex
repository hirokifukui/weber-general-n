For a prime $p$ and $r\ge1$, $|\operatorname{disc}\Q(\zeta_{p^r})|=p^{\,p^{r-1}(pr-r-1)}$ and $\Z[\zeta_{p^r}]$ is the ring of integers of $\Q(\zeta_{p^r})$ (\cite{Wash}, Chapter~2). In particular $|\operatorname{disc}\Q(\zeta_{3^r})|=3^{3^{r-1}(2r-1)}$, and this equals $\prod_{b\ne b'}|\xi^{b}-\xi^{b'}|$ over the $\varphi(3^r)$ primitive $3^r$-th roots of unity $\xi^b$, $b\in(\Z/3^r)^\times$, because the discriminant of the power basis $1,\zeta,\dots,\zeta^{\varphi(3^r)-1}$ is $\operatorname{disc}\Phi_{3^r}=\prod_{b\ne b'}(\xi^b-\xi^{b'})$ and the power basis is an integral basis.
