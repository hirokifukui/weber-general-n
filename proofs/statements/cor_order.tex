Put $k=1+m/2$ and
\[ T_n=\log2+\tfrac m2\log\tfrac4\pi+\tfrac12\log(2\pi k)+k(\log k-1)+\tfrac1{12k}+m\log\frac{U(2^{n+2})}{\log(2+\sqrt5)} . \]
Then $\log\widehat C_n\le T_n$, and for every odd prime $\ell$: $d_n(\ell)\log\ell>T_n$ implies $\ell\nmid k_n$.
