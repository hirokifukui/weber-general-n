All irreducible factors of $x^m+1$ over $\F_\ell$ have degree $d_n(\ell)=\ord_{2^n}(\ell)$. At $n=7$: $d=1$ for $\ell\equiv1\pmod{128}$ and $d=2$ for $\ell\equiv63,65,127\pmod{128}$.
