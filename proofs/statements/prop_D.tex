With $S(\chi)=\sum_{g\in G_n}\chi(g)\log|g\varepsilon_n|$ and $\chi$ ranging over the $m$ characters of $G_n$ with $\chi(\tau)=-1$, equivalently the even Dirichlet characters of conductor exactly $q$:
\[ D_n=2^{-m/2}\prod_{\chi}|S(\chi)|\qquad\text{and}\qquad D_n=2^{m(n+1)/2+1}\prod_{\chi}|L(1,\chi)| .\]
