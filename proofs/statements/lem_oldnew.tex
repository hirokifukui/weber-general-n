Let $L/K$ be a cyclic extension of number fields of degree $p^k$ and $\ell\ne p$ a prime. Let $\iota:\operatorname{Cl}(K)\to\operatorname{Cl}(L)$ be extension of ideals and $N:\operatorname{Cl}(L)\to\operatorname{Cl}(K)$ the norm. Then $\operatorname{Cl}(L)_\ell=\iota(\operatorname{Cl}(K)_\ell)\oplus\ker(N)_\ell$, $\iota$ is injective on $\operatorname{Cl}(K)_\ell$, and $v_\ell(h_L)>v_\ell(h_K)$ if and only if $\ker(N)$ has an element of order $\ell$. (In the Weber tower $k_n=h_n/h_{n-1}$ is an integer by the index formula of \cite{KY}, and $v_\ell(h_n)>v_\ell(h_{n-1})$ is then $\ell\mid k_n$.)
