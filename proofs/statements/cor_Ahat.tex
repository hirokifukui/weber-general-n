For even $q\ge4$ put $U(q)=\frac14(\log q+2\log2)+\log(4q)/\sqrt q$, and
\[ \widehat C_n=2\,(4/\pi)^{m/2}\,\Gamma(2+m/2)\,\big(U(2^{n+2})/\log(2+\sqrt5)\big)^{m}. \]
Then $C_n\le\widehat C_n$ for every $n\ge2$, and for every odd prime $\ell$: $\ell^{d_n(\ell)}>\widehat C_n$ implies $\ell\nmid k_n$.
