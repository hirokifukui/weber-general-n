Let $n\ge1$, $\ell\ne3$ an odd prime, $f\in\{1,2\}$ the order of $\ell$ modulo $3$, $3^{s}\,\|\,\ell^{2}-1$, $r=\min(n,s)$, $c=2\cdot3^{r-1}$, and $L^{\mathrm{rel}}_{3,n}$ as in Lemma~\ref{lem:mo25-z3}. Let $D_r^{(n)}>0$ be the covolume of Theorem~\ref{thm:rank3} and put
\[ C^{(3)}_{n,r}=(2/\pi)^{c/2}\,\Gamma(2+c/2)\,D_r^{(n)}\big/(L^{\mathrm{rel}}_{3,n})^{\,c} . \]
If $\ell^{f}>C^{(3)}_{n,r}$ then $\ell\nmid h_{3,n}/h_{3,n-1}$.
