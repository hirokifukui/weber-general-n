Let $n\ge2$, $\ell$ an odd prime and $d_n(\ell)=\ord_{2^n}(\ell)$. Let $D_n$ be the covolume of the log-lattice $H_n(\Z^m)$ of $A_n$ (\refDefH) and
\[ C_n=(2/\pi)^{m/2}\,\Gamma(2+m/2)\,D_n/L_n^{m}. \]
If $\ell^{d_n(\ell)}>C_n$ then $\ell\nmid k_n$.
