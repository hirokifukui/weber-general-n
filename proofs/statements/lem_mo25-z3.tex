If $\varepsilon\in E_{3,n}\setminus E_{3,n-1}$ and $\operatorname{Nr}_{B_{3,n}/B_{3,n-1}}(\varepsilon)=1$, then $\hgt(\varepsilon)\ge L^{\mathrm{rel}}_{3,n}:=\sqrt{3^n}\,\log\frac{3^{(3^n-1)/(2\cdot3^n)}+\sqrt{3^{(3^n-1)/3^n}+4}}2$, where $\hgt$ is the Euclidean norm of the log vector (\cite{MO16} Def.~2.2). (\cite{MO16} Lemma~2.5(2): Lemma~2.3 there, the Mahler-measure bound with $C=|\operatorname{Nr}_{B_{3,n}/\Q}(\varepsilon^2-1)|$, together with Lemma~2.4(2), $C\ge3^{(3^n-1)/2}$, which is \cite{MO13} Lemma~9.1.)
