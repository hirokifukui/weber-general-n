% conditions: ell_pm1_mod_81
If $\ell\equiv1\pmod{81}$ and $\ell>1.0728\times10^{33}$, or $\ell\equiv-1\pmod{81}$ and $\ell>3.2753\times10^{16}$, then $\ell\nmid h_{3,4}/h_{3,3}$. In the classes $81\,\|\,\ell\mp1$ these layer-fixed thresholds are below the uniform-in-$n$ bound of Morisawa and Okazaki, \cite{MO16} Thm.~A ($3.7111\times10^{37}$, resp.\ $6.0919\times10^{18}$), by the factors $3.4594\times10^{4}$, resp.\ $1.8599\times10^{2}$.
