With $\zeta=e^{2\pi i/q}$ and $X=\zeta+\zeta^{-1}$: $X+1=\zeta^{-1}(\zeta^3-1)/(\zeta-1)$, $X-1=\zeta^{-1}(\zeta^3+1)/(\zeta+1)$, hence $\varepsilon_n=(1-\zeta^3)^2(1-\zeta^2)/((1-\zeta)^2(1-\zeta^6))$; all factors are nonzero for $q\ge16$. Consequently $\log|\sigma_a\varepsilon_n|=2\log|1-\zeta^{3a}|-2\log|1-\zeta^a|+\log|1-\zeta^{2a}|-\log|1-\zeta^{6a}|$ for $a\in(\Z/q)^\times$.
