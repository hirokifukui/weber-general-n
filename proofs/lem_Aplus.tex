Let $u\in\RE\setminus\{\pm1\}$. By Lemma~\ref{lem:A}, $u\notin E_{n-1}$, and $\Nr u=1$ by the definition of $\RE$. These are exactly the two hypotheses of \cite{MO16} Lemma~2.5(1) (\S3.3 of \cite{MO16}, p.~817: $\varepsilon\in E_n\setminus E_{n-1}$ with $\mathrm{Nr}_{B_n/B_{n-1}}(\varepsilon)=1$), whose conclusion is $\hgt(u)\ge\sqrt{2^n}\log(2+\sqrt5)=L_n$.
