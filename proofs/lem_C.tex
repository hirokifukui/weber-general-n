Since $x^m+1$ is separable mod $\ell$, $\F_\ell[x]/(x^m+1)=\prod_g\F_\ell[x]/(g)$ over the irreducible factors. $w=(x^m+1)/f$ vanishes in every $g$-component with $g\ne f$ and is a unit in the $f$-component, so $M_f=0\times\dots\times\F_\ell[x]/(f)\times\dots\times0$ has dimension $d_f$, and $R_n/L_f\cong(\F_\ell[x]/(x^m+1))/M_f$ has dimension $m-d_f$. The Lean statement \path{component_card_quotient} is the dimension count for the quotient $V/W$ of $\F_\ell$-spaces; the pullback identification $R_n/L_f\cong V/W$ along $\pi$ is the bookkeeping just described and is not formalised.
